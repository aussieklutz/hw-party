How to play

  1. Scan the QR — or open the link — and join the queue.
  2. When your turn comes, name your team and your four hogs.
  3. ◀ ▶ walk  ·  ▲ aim up  ·  J jump
  4. Steer the Pointer arrows, click to place, then hold FIRE.
  5. Last hedgehog standing wins. Good luck!
